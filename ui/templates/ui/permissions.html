Data categories and permissions

Each category of your data belongs to the core or to a module. Nothing can read a category without a grant from you, and every read is recorded on your statement. Recipients are simulated in this version; no data leaves this device.

What a grant covers: every current record of the category, with all its fields (notes included), from all of your history, not only records made after the grant. The start and end only limit when the recipient may read. One grant per recipient and category is in force at a time; revoking it ends that recipient's access to the category.
